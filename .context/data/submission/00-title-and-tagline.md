# Project title + tagline

## Title

**AURA — WebMCP Creative Studio**

(Alt short titles: **AURA Studio**, **AURA — Agent-Native Creative Studio**.)

## Tagline (one line)

> AURA turns AI video generation from a black box into a collaborative
> production studio where agents work as a creative crew and humans
> remain the executive producer.

## Tagline (medium)

> AURA is a WebMCP-native creative studio: 16 tools, 10 specialist
> agents, and a Human Veto — all visible, all driven by any agent that
> speaks WebMCP.

## Tagline (long, for the project subtitle)

> AURA exposes a complete AI video production crew — Director, Brand
> Strategist, Scriptwriter, Copywriter, Graphic Designer, Motion
> Graphics, Voiceover, Video Editor, Critic/QA, and Project Manager —
> as 14 WebMCP tools. Every agent's status is visible in a control
> room. Every artifact lands in a workspace. Every final cut waits on
> a human approval gate. Run it from the in-app Director, from a
> browser agent, or from a server-side agent over HTTP. Same tools.
> Same rules. Same human veto.

## What makes this title work for the judges

- **AURA** is short, memorable, and reads as "AI" + "aura" (the
  presence/feeling a creative team gives a project).
- **WebMCP** is in the title — the judges know immediately that the
  submission is grounded in the required technology.
- **Creative Studio** positions the product as a coherent production
  experience, not a tech demo.

## One-line positioning for the thumbnail / hero image

> "16 tools · 10 agents · 1 human veto"
